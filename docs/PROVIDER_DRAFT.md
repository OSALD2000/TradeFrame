## Applicability and publication

Assess the repository, download/release pages and any website together as the
actual public offer. A free download alone does not settle which identification
duties apply. [Section 5 DDG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ddg/__5.html)
sets provider information duties for the services within its scope.
[Section 18 MStV](https://www.gesetze-bayern.de/Content/Document/MStV-18)
also addresses offers beyond exclusively personal/family purposes. Their scopes
need assessment for this publisher; this draft claims neither compliance nor an
exemption. Sources checked on 5 November 2026.

Once the owner supplies and approves the applicable details, replace this draft
with the reviewed notice. Make required information clearly labeled and directly
reachable on the public offer; including a file only inside a download does not
establish that reachability. Coordinate the accessible
[privacy notice](PRIVACY_DRAFT.md) and [terms](TERMS_DRAFT.md), then resolve the
remaining [release gates](RELEASE.md#documentation-and-evidence-gate-for-the-first-public-release).
