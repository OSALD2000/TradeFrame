# TradeFrame terms of use — review draft

**Draft dated 5 November 2026 for preview 0.7.10. Not effective contractual terms
or a software license.** The intended offering is a free, personally maintained
desktop journal. Publisher identity and legal review are outstanding;
see the [release gates](RELEASE.md#documentation-and-evidence-gate-for-the-first-public-release).

## Provider and scope

**Pending:** insert the approved provider/contact from the

TradeFrame records chart screenshots, observations and trading setup answers.
It can save Markdown/PNG files and deliver copies to selected folder destinations
or a Microsoft OneNote notebook. It does not place orders, connect to a broker,
provide personalized investment recommendations or calculate the statistics
shown as Coming soon. Journal entries do not promise future trading results.

## Your content and destinations

Capture and share only content you are entitled to use, including any personal
data or chart content belonging to others. Review the selected source and
destinations before saving. Your journal contents are not licensed as part of
TradeFrame's software merely because you use the app.

Keep your own backups. Unsaved drafts are memory-only; optional cloud delivery
can fail independently of a successful local save. When Local journal is off,
the app still retains a local working copy for retries/follow-up. Do not treat
OneNote or Dropbox as a verified backup without checking the resulting content.
[Backup, update and deletion instructions](DATA.md)

Connecting a destination requires your own access to that service/folder.
External service terms, account policies, availability and sharing rules apply
separately. OneNote disconnect does not revoke the Microsoft grant or delete
pages; changing destinations does not move earlier notes. Importing a OneNote
snapshot restores originally sent values and does not merge subsequent edits.
[Connection behavior and limits](SYNC.md)

## Software rights and third-party components

The [TradeFrame Source-Available License 1.0](../LICENSE.md) governs the project
software. It permits personal/professional own use, modifications and free
redistribution, with restrictions on sales, paid bundles and paid hosted/managed
access. It preserves user-content and third-party rights. The license text is
authoritative; these draft terms do not expand or replace it. Its custom wording
has not received an independent legal review.

Bundled third-party software retains its own licenses, supplied in the packages'
Electron/Chromium notices and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`. The project's own terms
must not replace those licenses.
